## Semantic SEO: Beyond the Keyword

For a long time, ranking well in search meant matching the right keywords. That's not really how modern search works anymore — search engines increasingly rank on *meaning*, not just on which words appear on a page. This series is about that shift: what "semantic" search actually looks at, why keyword-stuffing stopped working, and what it means, practically, to write and structure content for a search engine that's trying to understand intent rather than count occurrences.

This one is deliberately not Rails-specific or code-heavy — it's for anyone who writes online, runs a website, or is just curious how search really works today.

## Semantic Search in Ruby on Rails

This series is the technical counterpart: what semantic search actually *is* under the hood — embeddings, vector similarity, nearest-neighbor lookups — and how to build it into a real Rails application from the ground up. [AI with Ruby]({% link _posts/2026-08-22-semantic-search-with-pgvector.markdown %}) already gave semantic search one episode, wiring pgvector into a RubyLLM chat history; this series goes further and deeper, on its own, as the concept deserves.
